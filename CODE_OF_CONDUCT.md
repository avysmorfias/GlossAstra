# GlossAstra Code of Conduct
## Our Pledge
We are committed to making GlossAstra a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive project for everyone who participates in good faith.

We welcome contributions regardless of personal characteristics, background, nationality, language, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, level of experience, or other personal circumstances.

Participation in GlossAstra should be based on mutual respect, constructive communication, and a shared interest in improving access to useful language resources.
## Expected Behavior
Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive community include:
- treating other people with respect and kindness;
- discussing ideas, sources, and classifications without attacking people;
- respecting different experiences, opinions, and areas of expertise;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them when appropriate;
- helping keep discussions useful, relevant, and welcoming.
## Unacceptable Behavior
The following behavior is not acceptable in GlossAstra's community spaces:
- harassment, threats, intimidation, or unwelcome personal attention;
- insulting, degrading, or personally attacking other participants;
- discriminatory or hateful behavior directed at individuals or groups;
- publishing someone's private or personal information without permission;
- deliberately disrupting discussions or repeatedly interfering with other participants' contributions;
- submitting deliberately misleading information, fabricated sources, or deceptive material;
- any other behavior that could reasonably be considered seriously inappropriate or harmful in the context of the project.
## Reporting
If you believe that someone has violated this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the GlossAstra maintainers at:
**[glossastra@proton.me](mailto:glossastra@proton.me)**

Please do not use a public GitHub issue to report harassment, personal information, or other sensitive conduct concerns.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as reasonably possible. Information concerning a report will be handled privately and shared only when necessary to address the situation.
## Enforcement
GlossAstra maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or otherwise moderate contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the circumstances, possible actions may include:
1. a private reminder or correction;
2. a warning;
3. temporary restriction or removal from project spaces;
4. permanent restriction or removal in serious or repeated cases.

The appropriate response depends on the circumstances and seriousness of the behavior. These measures are guidelines rather than a mandatory sequence.
## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to participation in the GlossAstra repository and to any official GlossAstra community spaces or activities.

It also applies when someone is officially representing GlossAstra in another public or online space.
