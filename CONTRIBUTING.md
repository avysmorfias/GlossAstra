# Contributing to GlossAstra
Thank you for helping improve GlossAstra.

GlossAstra is a curated catalog of resources for human languages. Contributions are welcome from anyone who can help add, verify, correct, or improve language and resource information.

## What You Can Contribute
You can help by:
* adding a missing language or resource;
* correcting inaccurate or outdated information;
* reporting or fixing broken links;
* improving resource descriptions or categorization;
* identifying duplicate entries;
* suggesting useful resources that are currently missing.

## Before Contributing
Please search the repository first to make sure the language, resource, or issue is not already covered.

When possible, use the original, official, or most authoritative source for a resource.

Please avoid adding resources based only on search results or secondary references when a more direct source is available.

## Adding Resources
Add resources to the most appropriate language or variety page and category.

Use the resource name as the link text whenever possible.

Keep descriptions short, clear, and neutral. Avoid promotional language, personal ratings, or unsupported claims.

When relevant, mention the main language or languages used by the resource.

For example:
```markdown
- [Introductory Mansi Language Course](https://example.org/course) — Russian. Introductory course for learners.
```

Follow the existing category structure where possible. If a resource does not fit an existing category, explain the proposed change or open an issue before creating a new category.

## Adding or Correcting Language Information
When adding language names, codes, classifications, or other factual information, prefer authoritative sources such as language catalogues, academic institutions, official language organizations, or established research databases.

If sources disagree, do not silently present one classification as universally correct. Identify the relevant source or classification system and make the distinction clear.

Do not invent language codes, classifications, names, or relationships between languages or varieties.

## Copyright and Third-Party Materials
GlossAstra is primarily a catalog of resources and links to materials hosted elsewhere.

Do not upload copyrighted third-party books, recordings, courses, datasets, or other materials unless redistribution is explicitly permitted.

Whenever possible, link to the original, official, or authorized source instead.

A resource being freely accessible online does not necessarily mean that it is free to redistribute.

Third-party resources remain subject to their own copyright, licenses, and terms of use.
## Issues
Please open an issue when you:
* find a broken link;
* find incorrect or outdated information;
* want to suggest a resource;
* notice a duplicate or classification problem;
* have a copyright or attribution concern;
* are unsure how something should be added.

Please check existing issues before opening a new one.

Use the available issue template when it matches your situation.
## Pull Requests
Pull requests are welcome for direct additions and corrections.

Please:
* use a clear and descriptive title;
* keep each pull request focused on one logical change when possible;
* explain what was added or changed;
* check links before submitting;
* check spelling and Markdown formatting.

Small corrections are welcome. You do not need to make a large contribution to help improve GlossAstra.
## Quality and Scope
GlossAstra prioritizes useful, relevant, and discoverable resources, with particular attention to lesser-resourced and hard-to-find languages, but it is not limited to them.

Historical or inactive resources may still be included when they have educational, linguistic, cultural, research, or historical value.

Community resources, social platforms, media, software, and other less formal resources may also be included when they provide meaningful access to or use of a language.

Please avoid spam, promotional-only pages, duplicate entries, and resources with unclear provenance.
## Accuracy
GlossAstra is a curated, community-contributed catalog and may contain mistakes.

If you are more knowledgeable about a language, its classification, terminology, or resources, please help us correct the information rather than assuming that the existing entry is authoritative.

When making corrections, provide a reliable source whenever possible.
