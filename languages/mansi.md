# Mansi

> Mansi belongs to the Ob-Ugric branch of the Uralic language family. Its internal classification differs between linguistic traditions and language catalogues.

**Variety pages:**  
[Northern Mansi](northern-mansi.md) · [Eastern Mansi](eastern-mansi.md) · [Western Mansi](western-mansi.md) · [Southern Mansi](southern-mansi.md)

The four pages above are GlossAstra's organizational pages for resource discovery. They do not imply that all four groups have the same classification level in external sources.

**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-02

---
## Classification at a glance

|Source|Main structure|
|---|---|
|**Ob-Ugric Institute, 2024**|4 dialect groups: Northern, Eastern, Western, Southern|
|**Atlas of Indigenous Peoples**|Northern Mansi, Middle Mansi, and Southern Mansi; Eastern and Western Mansi are described as dialect groups within Middle Mansi|
|**Glottolog 5.3**|3 Spoken L1 Languages: Northern Mansi, Central Mansi, Southern Mansi; Central Mansi contains West Mansi and East Mansi dialects|
|**Endangered Languages Project**|3 relevant language entries: Northern Mansi, Eastern Mansi, Western Mansi|

These systems use different classification levels, so the same variety may be called a language, dialect group, dialect, or subdialect depending on the source.

## 1. Russian dialectological model

The 2024 _Rules of Orthography and Punctuation of the Mansi Language (Sosva dialect)_ describe four dialect groups and their internal dialects and local varieties.

```text
Mansi
├── Northern dialect group
│   ├── Sosva dialect
│   │   ├── Upper Sosva
│   │   ├── Middle and Lower Sosva
│   │   ├── Lyapin
│   │   └── Ob
│   └── Upper Lozva dialect
│
├── Eastern dialect group
│   └── Konda dialect
│       ├── Upper Konda
│       ├── Middle Konda
│       ├── Lower Konda
│       └── Karym
│
├── Western dialect group
│   ├── Pelym
│   ├── Vagil
│   ├── Middle Lozva
│   └── Lower Lozva
│
└── Southern dialect group
    └── Tavda
```

**Source:** [Ob-Ugric Institute — _Rules of Orthography and Punctuation of the Mansi Language (Sosva dialect)_, 2024](https://ouipiir.ru/sites/default/files/%D0%9F%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%B8%D0%BB%D0%B0%20%D0%BE%D1%80%D1%84%D0%BE%D0%B3%20%D0%BC%D0%B0%D0%BD%D1%81%D0%B8%20%D1%8F%D0%B7%20-%20%D0%93%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%81%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0%20%D0%94%D0%B8%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%81%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0.pdf)

## 2. Atlas of Indigenous Peoples

The Atlas describes the older four-part division, but its current description places Eastern and Western Mansi together as **Middle Mansi**.

```text
Mansi
├── Northern Mansi
│   ├── Sosva
│   ├── Sygva / Lyapin
│   ├── Upper Lozva
│   └── Ob
│
├── Middle Mansi
│   ├── Eastern group
│   │   ├── Upper Konda
│   │   ├── Middle Konda
│   │   ├── Lower Konda
│   │   └── Yukonda
│   │
│   └── Western group
│       ├── Middle Lozva
│       ├── Lower Lozva
│       ├── Pelym
│       └── Vagil
│
└── Southern Mansi
    └── Tavda
```

**Source:** [Atlas of Indigenous Peoples of the North, Siberia and the Far East — Mansi language](https://atlaskmns.ru/page/ru/lang_mansi_all.html)

The Atlas states that Eastern and Western Mansi have recently been united into a single Middle Mansi language, while retaining Eastern and Western dialect groups within it.

## 3. Glottolog 5.3

Glottolog uses a different hierarchy. `Northern Mansi`, `Central Mansi`, and `Southern Mansi` are **Spoken L1 Languages**. `West Mansi` and `East Mansi` are **Dialects** within Central Mansi.

```text
Mansic — Family
├── North-Central Mansi — Family
│   │
│   ├── Northern Mansi — Spoken L1 Language
│   │   ├── Sosva Mansi
│   │   ├── Sygva Mansi
│   │   ├── Ob Mansi
│   │   └── Upper Loz'va Mansi
│   │
│   └── Central Mansi — Spoken L1 Language
│       │
│       ├── West Mansi — Dialect
│       │   ├── Middle Lozva
│       │   ├── Lower Lozva
│       │   ├── North Vagil
│       │   ├── Pelym
│       │   └── South Vagil
│       │
│       └── East Mansi — Dialect
│           ├── Upper Konda
│           ├── Middle Konda
│           ├── Lower Konda
│           └── Jukonda
│
└── Southern Mansi — Spoken L1 Language
```
