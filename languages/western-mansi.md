# Western Mansi

> Western Mansi is the western dialect group of Mansi.

For the broader classification of Mansi and a comparison of classification systems, see [Mansi](mansi.md).

**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-02

## Corpora & Language Data
* [Ob-BABEL Ob-Ugric Text Corpus](https://www.babel.gwi.uni-muenchen.de/index.php?abfrage=all_mansi_corpus&subnavi=corpus_pub%2F) — searchable corpus containing Western Mansi materials from Pelym, Northern Vagilsk and Middle Lozva, with glossed texts, translations and additional linguistic information. The broader Ob-Ugric Database also covers Lower Lozva materials.
## Research & Publications
* [On Dative-Lative Encoded Direct Objects in West Mansi](https://kirj.ee/wp-content/plugins/kirj/pub/ling-2023-4-307-338_20231211135718.pdf) — Gerson Klumpp (2023), *Linguistica Uralica* 59(4), pp. 307–338. A study of differential object marking in West Mansi, based on Pelymka, North Vagilsk and Lozva materials.
## Notes
Sources differ in how finely the Vagil variety is divided. Glottolog distinguishes North Vagil and South Vagil, while other sources may refer simply to Vagil or Vagil'sk.

The Western Mansi dialect group is extinct, and the available corpus documentation is based largely on historical text materials.

See [Mansi](mansi.md) for a comparison of classification systems.
