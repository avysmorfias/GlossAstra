# Northern Mansi

> Northern Mansi is the northern dialect group of Mansi. The Sosva dialect forms the basis of the modern literary Mansi language.

For the broader classification of Mansi and a comparison of classification systems, see [Mansi](mansi.md).

**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-02

## Classification diagram
The following diagram is a simplified organizational overview. Sources differ in how they assign some varieties to levels such as dialects and subdialects.

```text
Northern Mansi
├── Sosva dialect
│   ├── Upper Sosva
│   ├── Middle and Lower Sosva
│   ├── Sygva / Lyapin
│   └── Ob
└── Upper Lozva dialect
```

## Learning & Teaching
* [Northern Mansi Basic Course](https://tuhat.helsinki.fi/ws/portalfiles/portal/172903753/Virtanen_Horvath_Merova_Pohjoismansin_peruskurssi_17082021.pdf) — course materials for Northern Mansi by Susanna Virtanen, Csilla Horváth and Tamara Merova.
* [Лылыӈ ла̄тыӈ: Mansi Textbook for Beginners](https://doi.org/10.33341/sus.1436) — contemporary Mansi textbook for non-native beginners by Susanna Virtanen, Csilla Horváth and Tamara Merova (2025). It consists of fifteen chapters with basic structures, vocabulary, texts and exercises.
* [Practical Course of the Mansi Language](https://www.babel.gwi.uni-muenchen.de/index.php?abfrage=TM_MA) — revised edition of *Practical Mansi Language Course, Part 1* by E. K. Skribnik and K. V. Afanasyeva. Includes eight units, audio materials, exercise answers and a Mansi–Russian glossary.
* [Mansi: Types of Linguistic Analysis](https://ouipiir.ru/content/%D0%BC%D0%B0%D0%BD%D1%81%D0%B8%D0%B9%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B9-%D1%8F%D0%B7%D1%8B%D0%BA-%D0%B2%D0%B8%D0%B4%D1%8B-%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B7%D0%B1%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2) — teaching material covering phonetic, morphological, morphemic and syntactic analysis.

## Notes
Glottolog treats Sosva, Sygva, Ob and Upper Loz'va as dialects of Northern Mansi. Russian descriptions may place Sygva and Ob at a different level within the northern group. See [Mansi](mansi.md) for the comparison of classifications.

The list prioritizes resources with direct relevance to Northern Mansi. General Mansi resources are included when they are important for the modern literary language or contain substantial Northern Mansi material.

GitHub repositories are listed as resources for language technology and research. A repository may contain source code or linguistic data rather than a ready-to-use application.

The presence of a third-party book, corpus or dataset in this catalogue does not imply that its files may be freely redistributed. See the original source for copyright and licence conditions.
