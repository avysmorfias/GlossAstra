# Northern Mansi

> Northern Mansi is the northern dialect group of Mansi. The Sosva dialect forms the basis of the modern literary Mansi language.

For the broader classification of Mansi and a comparison of classification systems, see [Mansi](mansi.md).

## Classification

The following diagram is a simplified organizational overview. Sources differ in how they assign some varieties to levels such as dialects and subdialects.

```text
Northern Mansi
├── Sosva dialect
│   ├── Upper Sosva
│   ├── Middle and Lower Sosva
│   ├── Sygva / Lyapin
│   └── Ob
└── Upper Lozva dialect
```

## Corpora & Language Data

* [OUDB Northern Mansi Corpus](https://www.oudb.gwi.uni-muenchen.de/index.php?abfrage=NM_corpus&language=en) — searchable Northern Mansi corpus containing published texts, archival material and texts from *Luima Seripos*.
* [INEL Mansi Corpus](https://inel.corpora.uni-hamburg.de/MansiCorpus/) — annotated corpus with online search and download; most of the data come from the Northern dialect group. A [downloadable release](https://www.fdr.uni-hamburg.de/record/18715) is also available.
* [mansi-team/mansi_corpus](https://github.com/mansi-team/mansi_corpus) — Mansi corpus repository based on the Tsakorpus corpus-search platform.

## Language Technology & Digital Tools

* [Yandex Translate](https://translate.yandex.ru/) — Mansi is listed among the service's supported languages.
- [Mansi–Russian Dictionary — FU-Lab](https://dict.fu-lab.ru/dict?id=238837) — Online interface to the School Mansi–Russian (Orthographic) Dictionary by K. V. Afanasyeva and S. A. Sobyanina (2012). Contains 3,907 terms. Available for online lookup; no downloadable copy is linked from the page.
- [Northern Mansi Dictionary — Kaikki](https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Northern%20Mansi/index.html) — Machine-readable Northern Mansi dictionary based on structured data extracted from Wiktionary and processed with Wiktextract. Provides online word lookup, browsing and downloadable lexical data in JSONL format.
- [Uralonet](https://www.uralonet.nytud.hu/search.cgi?locale=en_GB) — Uralic etymological database containing reconstructed protoforms, cognate comparisons, borrowing relations and etymological explanations. Includes Mansi/Vogul data and is useful for historical-comparative linguistic research.
- [Wiktionary — Northern Mansi](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Northern_Mansi_language) — Community-maintained index of Northern Mansi lexical entries, lemmas and language-specific editorial guidelines. Useful for exploring and contributing to Wiktionary's Mansi vocabulary.
- [GiellaLT: Mansi Dictionary Data](https://github.com/giellalt/dict-mns-mul) — Open-source lexical data and source files for an electronic Mansi dictionary project. The repository describes Mansi–Hungarian, Mansi–Russian and Mansi–English dictionary data. Licensed under CC BY 4.0.
* [giellalt/lang-mns](https://github.com/giellalt/lang-mns) — GiellaLT repository for Northern Mansi morphology, analysers, proofing tools and language resources.
* [Northern Mansi Documentation](https://giellalt.uit.no/lang/mns/NorthernMansiDocumentation.html) — technical documentation for Northern Mansi morphological analysers.
* [GiellaLT Mansi Documentation](https://giellalt.github.io/lang-mns/) — documentation and developer resources for Mansi language technology.
* [giellalt/keyboard-mns](https://github.com/giellalt/keyboard-mns) — GiellaLT keyboard definitions for Mansi, with desktop and mobile targets.

## Notes

Glottolog treats Sosva, Sygva, Ob and Upper Loz'va as dialects of Northern Mansi. Russian descriptions may place Sygva and Ob at a different level within the northern group. The broader comparison of classification systems is provided on the main Mansi page.

The list prioritizes resources with direct relevance to Northern Mansi. General Mansi resources are included when they are important for the modern literary language or contain substantial Northern Mansi material.

GitHub repositories are listed as resources for language technology and research. A repository may contain source code or linguistic data rather than a ready-to-use application.

The presence of a third-party book, corpus or dataset in this catalogue does not imply that its files may be freely redistributed. See the original source for copyright and licence conditions.
