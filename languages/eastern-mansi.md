# Eastern Mansi

> Eastern Mansi is the eastern (Konda) dialect group of Mansi.

For the broader classification of Mansi and a comparison of classification systems, see [Mansi](mansi.md).

**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-02

## Classification diagram
The following diagram is a simplified organizational overview. Sources differ in the terminology and level used for some varieties.

```text
Eastern Mansi
└── Konda / Eastern group
    ├── Upper Konda
    ├── Middle Konda
    ├── Lower Konda
    └── Jukonda / Yukonda
```

## Grammar & Reference
* [Eastern Mansi (Konda) Grammar](https://babel.gwi.uni-muenchen.de/media/downloads/grammar/EasternMansi/EasternMansiGrammar.pdf) — Ulla-Maija Forsberg (2007), a detailed grammatical description of Middle and Lower Konda Mansi based primarily on texts and grammatical notes collected by Artturi Kannisto.
## Books & Historical Texts
* [Das Evangelium Matthäi in den Dialect der kondischen Wogulen im Gouvernement Tobolsk](https://www.finna.fi/Record/fikka.90892) — translation of the Gospel of Matthew into Konda Mansi by G. Popov, published in London in 1868. The catalogue record lists 116 pages.
## Notes
Glottolog uses the name **East Mansi** for this dialect and places it under Central Mansi. Russian sources commonly use **Eastern Mansi** or **Konda dialect**.

The name **Karym** also appears in descriptions of the eastern Mansi area. It is associated with the Konda/Jukonda area in some sources, but Glottolog does not provide a separate Karym node, so it is not listed here as a separate coded variety.

The available Eastern Mansi documentation is largely historical. The grammar describes a language form that was already no longer spoken as such by the beginning of the 21st century.

See [Mansi](mansi.md) for a comparison of classification systems.
