# Southern Mansi

> Southern Mansi is the southern (Tavda) dialect group of Mansi in the four-part Russian classification.

For the broader classification of Mansi and a comparison of classification systems, see [Mansi](mansi.md).

## Corpora & Language Data
* [INEL Tavda Mansi Corpus](https://www.fdr.uni-hamburg.de/record/17513) — annotated corpus of Tavda Mansi with online search, downloadable data, interlinear glosses, translations and grammatical annotation. The corpus identifies Tavda as the Southern dialect group and includes several local Tavda subdialects. An [online search interface](https://inel.corpora.uni-hamburg.de/TavdaMansiCorpus/search) is also available.
## Websites & Online Resources
* [Tavda Mansi Language](https://norbertszilagyi91.wixsite.com/tawdamansi) — research website by Norbert Szilágyi containing materials on Tavda Mansi, including analysed texts and linguistic descriptions. Its materials are also included in the INEL corpus for comparison, with differences in analysis preserved.
## Grammar & Reference
* [System der paradigmatischen Suffixmorpheme des wogulischen Dialektes an der Tawda](https://doi.org/10.1515/9783111358734) — László Honti (1975), a detailed study of the Tavda Mansi dialect, focusing on morphophonology, morphology and paradigmatic suffix morphemes.
## Notes
Glottolog treats Southern Mansi as a separate Spoken L1 Language with the Glottocode `sout3253` and currently marks it as extinct.

The Southern Mansi variety is generally referred to as **Tavda Mansi** because of its traditional distribution along the Tavda River. The Tavda variety became extinct in the 20th century.

Glottolog's Southern Mansi entry currently links to the Endangered Languages Project's Western Mansi entry rather than to a separate Southern Mansi entry. No ELP identifier is therefore assigned here.

See [Mansi](mansi.md) for a comparison of classification systems.
