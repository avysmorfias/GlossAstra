## Institutions & General Portals

### Language Institutions & Organizations

* [Akademio de Esperanto](https://www.akademio-de-esperanto.org/) — independent language institution concerned with the fundamental principles and development of Esperanto; it issues official decisions on language elements and publishes recommendations on usage.
* [Universala Esperanto-Asocio (UEA)](https://uea.org/) — international organization of the Esperanto movement, with publications, events, services and community activities.
* [Tutmonda Esperantista Junulara Organizo (TEJO)](https://www.tejo.org/) — international youth organization for Esperanto speakers and part of the UEA movement.

### General Portals & Websites

* [Esperanto.net](https://esperanto.net/en/) — UEA's general multilingual portal about Esperanto, its history, use, learning, literature and movement.
* [Esperanto Wikipedia](https://eo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esperanto) — general encyclopedia article in Esperanto with further references.
* [Esperantopedia](https://esperantopedia.org/ru/) — multilingual portal about Esperanto with a Russian-language interface and sections on the language, grammar, vocabulary, phrases and digital tools.

## Language Standard, Grammar & Reference

* [Fundamento de Esperanto](https://www.akademio-de-esperanto.org/fundamento/) — foundational standard text of Esperanto, including the Antaŭparolo, 16-rule grammar, *Ekzercaro* and *Universala Vortaro*. The Fundamento was established as the basic law of Esperanto at the 1905 Boulogne Congress.
* [Akademia Vortaro](https://www.akademio-de-esperanto.org/akademia_vortaro/) — searchable reference to the vocabulary of the *Fundamento* and the official additions to the *Universala Vortaro*.
* [Official Additions to the Universal Dictionary](https://www.akademio-de-esperanto.org/verkoj/oficialaj_aldonoj.html) — official additions to the *Universala Vortaro*, including the current series through 10OA (2023).
* [Lingva Konsultejo](https://www.akademio-de-esperanto.org/akademio/index.php?title=Lingva_Konsultejo) — language consultation service of the Akademio de Esperanto for questions about grammar, word formation and usage. Its published answers are recommendations rather than formal decisions of the Akademio.
* [Plena Manlibro de Esperanta Gramatiko (PMEG)](https://bertilow.com/pmeg/) — extensive non-official practical reference covering grammar, pronunciation, word formation, spelling and computer-related writing conventions.

## Dictionaries & Lexical Resources

* [Reta Vortaro (ReVo)](https://reta-vortaro.de/revo/) — major online Esperanto dictionary with definitions and examples in Esperanto.
* [Plena Ilustrita Vortaro de Esperanto (PIV)](https://vortaro.net/) — major general Esperanto dictionary; the current online edition is PIV 2020.
* [Lernu! Dictionary](https://lernu.net/vortaro) — multilingual bidirectional Esperanto dictionary integrated into the Lernu! learning platform.
* [Komputeko](https://komputeko.net/) — specialized Esperanto terminology database for computing and information technology.

## Learning Resources

### Courses & Learning Platforms

* [Lernu!](https://lernu.net/?hl=ru) — multilingual platform with free Esperanto courses, interactive lessons, grammar, media, a dictionary, a forum and teaching materials. The link opens the Russian-language interface.
* [Эсперанто-гавань](https://esperanto.su/) — Russian-language learning website with grammar explanations, interactive word-building and vocabulary games, and supplementary materials for the Esperanto textbook by D. A. Shevchenko and A. O. Striganova.
* [Edukado.net](https://edukado.net/) — resource centre for Esperanto teaching, with teaching materials, courses, examinations and a professional community.
* [Kurso de Esperanto](https://www.kurso.com.br/) — free downloadable multimedia self-study program covering the basics of Esperanto in 12 lessons, with pronunciation and listening exercises, songs and other practice activities. Downloads are offered for Windows, macOS and Linux.
* [Esperanto 12 — Learn Esperanto in 12 Hours](https://esperanto12.net/ru/) — free multilingual introductory course based on the Zagreb method. It focuses on 500 essential words and basic conversational skills and can be used without registration.
* [Saluton — free Esperanto course](https://esperantopedia.org/ru/learn/course/) — free beginner course on Esperantopedia, currently organised into 14 lessons and four review units, with a story, grammar explanations and self-checking exercises. The course has a Russian-language interface.

### Speaking Practice

* [Ekparolu!](https://edukado.net/ekparolu/prezento) — free conversation-practice programme within Edukado.net. It connects learners with a basic knowledge of Esperanto with experienced speakers for online conversation sessions; the project page describes up to ten opportunities to practise with different speakers.

### Phrasebooks & Practice Materials

* [50languages — Russian to Esperanto phrasebook](https://www.50languages.com/ru/learn/phrasebook/eo) — Russian-language online phrasebook with themed collections of everyday phrases in Esperanto and audio support.

### Learning Apps & Mobile Courses

* [Duolingo — Esperanto](https://www.duolingo.com/course/eo/en/Learn-Esperanto) — interactive Esperanto course with lessons and practice activities; this course uses English as its base language.
* [Drops — Esperanto](https://languagedrops.com/language/learn-esperanto) — vocabulary-focused Esperanto course in the Drops app, using illustrated words and short daily practice sessions.
* [Saluton — iPhone app (App Store)](https://apps.apple.com/ru/app/saluton-%D1%83%D1%87%D0%B8%D1%82%D1%8C-%D1%8D%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%BD%D1%82%D0%BE/id6821067582) — mobile version of the Saluton course with step-by-step lessons, pronunciation recordings, pictograms, review activities and flashcards. The App Store listing currently describes lessons 1–12; the web course currently has 14 lessons. Available for iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
* [Learn Esperanto — 50 languages (Android)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.goethe.eo) — Android app with 30 Esperanto lessons, combining audio and text for vocabulary and everyday phrases.

## Notes

This page is intentionally selective. It focuses on major official, institutional, widely used, specialized or otherwise especially useful resources rather than attempting to catalogue every Esperanto website, course, application, video channel or community.

Official and institutional resources are distinguished from independent community projects where relevant. In particular, the Akademio de Esperanto is the language institution responsible for official language decisions, while resources such as PMEG, ReVo and the various learning platforms are independent reference or educational projects.

Third-party resources remain subject to their own copyright, licences and terms of use. GlossAstra primarily catalogs and links to resources hosted by their respective publishers, institutions or projects rather than redistributing their contents.
