> A constructed international auxiliary language created by L. L. Zamenhof and first published in 1887.

## Language Institutions & Movement
* [Akademio de Esperanto](https://www.akademio-de-esperanto.org/) — independent language institution concerned with the fundamental principles and development of Esperanto, including official language decisions and recommendations.
* [Universala Esperanto-Asocio (UEA)](https://uea.org/) — international organization of the Esperanto movement, with publications, events, services and community activities.
* [Tutmonda Esperantista Junulara Organizo (TEJO)](https://www.tejo.org/) — international youth organization for Esperanto speakers and part of the UEA movement.
## General Portals

## Language Standard & Reference
* [Fundamento de Esperanto](https://www.akademio-de-esperanto.org/fundamento/) — foundational standard text of Esperanto, including the Antaŭparolo, 16-rule grammar, *Ekzercaro* and *Universala Vortaro*. The Fundamento was established as the basic law of Esperanto at the 1905 Boulogne Congress.
* [Akademia Vortaro](https://www.akademio-de-esperanto.org/akademia_vortaro/) — searchable reference containing the Fundamental vocabulary together with officially added elements and related corrections.
* [Official Additions to the Universal Dictionary](https://www.akademio-de-esperanto.org/verkoj/oficialaj_aldonoj.html) — official additions to the *Universala Vortaro*, including the current series through 10OA.
* [Lingva Konsultejo](https://www.akademio-de-esperanto.org/akademio/index.php?title=Lingva_Konsultejo) — language consultation service of the Akademio de Esperanto for questions about grammar, word formation and usage. Its published answers are recommendations rather than formal decisions of the Akademio.
* [Plena Manlibro de Esperanta Gramatiko (PMEG)](https://bertilow.com/pmeg/) — extensive non-official practical reference covering grammar, pronunciation, word formation, spelling and computer-related writing conventions.

## Notes
This page is intentionally selective. It focuses on major official, institutional, widely used, specialized or otherwise especially useful resources rather than attempting to catalogue every Esperanto website, course, application, video channel or community.

Official and institutional resources are distinguished from independent community projects where relevant. In particular, the Akademio de Esperanto is the language institution responsible for official language decisions, while resources such as PMEG, ReVo and the various learning platforms are independent reference or educational projects.

Third-party resources remain subject to their own copyright, licences and terms of use. GlossAstra primarily catalogs and links to resources hosted by their respective publishers, institutions or projects rather than redistributing their contents.
