<h1 align="center">GlossAstra</h1>

> A curated collection of resources for human languages, with a focus on lesser-resourced and hard-to-find materials.

Useful language resources are often scattered across books, archives, websites, video platforms, GitHub repositories, community channels, and other places. GlossAstra brings information about these resources together in one catalog.

The project includes many kinds of language-related materials: books and dictionaries, grammars and courses, audio and video recordings, corpora and datasets, software and digital tools, archives, websites, communities, and other useful projects and materials.

GlossAstra focuses especially on languages whose resources are difficult to discover or are available in limited numbers, but it is not limited to them. The catalog is intended to grow gradually and is not an attempt to list every resource for every language.

## What you can find

GlossAstra may contain links or references to:

- books, dictionaries, grammars, and other texts
- courses and learning materials
- audio recordings, video lessons, lectures, and documentaries
- corpora, datasets, and research materials
- websites, online dictionaries, and databases
- software, GitHub projects, language tools, and speech technology
- digital libraries and archives
- language organizations, communities, Telegram channels, forums, and other social media
- other language-related materials, including community-created resources

A resource does not have to be hosted on GitHub or be freely downloadable to be included.

## Catalog, not a file repository

GlossAstra primarily catalogs and links to resources hosted elsewhere. It is also an overview of what resources exist for a language.

A resource may therefore be listed even when it is difficult to access, available only in print, behind a paywall, or no longer hosted at its original location. In such cases, GlossAstra may simply document that the resource exists and provide enough information to help locate it elsewhere.

GlossAstra does not redistribute copyrighted third-party materials unless their licenses explicitly allow redistribution.

Listing a resource does not imply ownership, endorsement, or permission to reuse it. Please refer to the original source for the resource's copyright, license, and terms of use.

## Contributing

Contributions are welcome.

You can help by:

- adding a missing resource or language
- correcting inaccurate information
- reporting broken or outdated links
- improving descriptions or categorization
- pointing out duplicates or other problems

You do not need to be a programmer to contribute. Suggestions and corrections can also be submitted through GitHub Issues.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for contribution guidelines, or [open an issue](../../issues).

GlossAstra is a growing catalog and may contain mistakes or incomplete information. Corrections and additional sources are welcome.
